Our Story

Rooted in tradition, inspired by the seasons

At Ember & Oak, we believe great food begins with great ingredients. Our kitchen works closely with local farmers and foragers to bring the best of each season to your plate.

Every dish is kissed by our wood-fired oven and grill — techniques passed down through generations, reimagined for the modern table.
