## 1. Why "Nama" — Brand Story

### Etymology & Emotional Hook

**نما (Nama)** in Arabic means *to grow, flourish, thrive*. It is not a foreign word forced into Arabic — it is a native root that Saudi women already feel in their language:

- نما شعري → my hair grew
- نما جمالها → her beauty flourished
- نما واثقة → she grew confident

This is the entire brand promise in one syllable: **real growth from within, not fake promises on the outside.**

### Brand Hierarchy

| Layer | English | Arabic | Usage |
|-------|---------|--------|-------|
| **Legal/Store name** | Nama Beauty | نما بيوتي | Footer, invoices, policies |
| **Short brand** | Nama | نما | Logo, headlines, social |
| **Tagline** | Beauty that grows from within | جمال ينمو من الداخل | Hero, ads, packaging |
| **Domain** | namabeauty.shop | — | Primary URL (LOCKED) |

### Visual Identity Direction

- **Tone:** Clean, clinical-but-warm, premium Saudi wellness (think: Nahdi premium aisle meets Glossier science)
- **Colors:** Soft sand (#F5F0EB), deep olive green (#3D5A3E) = growth/nature, warm gold accent (#C9A96E) = premium
- **Typography:** Modern Arabic (Tajawal or IBM Plex Arabic) + clean Latin for ingredient names
- **Photography:** Real Saudi women (hijabi and non-hijabi), natural light, desert-modern aesthetic — never stock Chinese factory vibes
- **Trust signals everywhere:** SFDA path, ingredient transparency, "صنع وفق معايير GMP", batch numbers, before/after disclaimers (no medical claims)

## 2. Niche A — Locked

### Niche Name

**Women's Inner Beauty & Daily Wellness**  
**الجمال الداخلي والعافية اليومية للمرأة السعودية**

### Why This Niche Wins

| Factor | Data / Logic |
|--------|-------------|
| **Market size** | Saudi beauty & personal care: ~$5.95B (2024), growing 4.2% annually. Supplements/collagen segment growing 8–12% CAGR. |
| **Audience** | 60%+ population under 30. Women 22–45 in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam = massive, affluent, mobile-first. |
| **COD fit** | Premium ingestible beauty AOV 150–350 SAR = high margin, low return vs. fashion (20–30% returns). Home/wellness RTO typically under 8%. |
| **Impulse + Confirmation** | Problems are emotionally painful (hair falling, can't sleep, dull skin) BUT solutions are food supplements — no "embarrassing" product shame at delivery. |
| **Differentiation** | Everyone sells the same Chinese hair serum, whitening cream, and minoxidil drops. Almost nobody sells a **branded, science-backed, Arabic-first supplement line** with premium positioning. |
| **Regulatory path** | Food supplements (SFDA) — no GHAD cosmetics paperwork for creams. Avoid drug claims. |
| **Vision 2030 tailwind** | Women in workforce, fitness culture, self-care normalization, social media beauty inspiration (Chalhoub: Saudi women are the most engaged makeup/wellness consumers in GCC). |

### The Big Niche Rule

This is ONE umbrella niche with THREE different problems — not three complementary products for the same problem. Each product stands alone but cross-sells naturally because the same woman has all three problems.

```

                    │     NAMA BEAUTY (نما بيوتي)    │

| **Marketing Name (AR)** | **أمالج نما للشعر** — Nama Hair Strength Gummies |
| **Subtitle (AR)** | بيوتين + كولاجين بحري + كيراتين + زنك |
| **English descriptor** | Biotin + Marine Collagen + Keratin + Zinc Gummies |
| **Format** | Gummies (60 count, 30-day supply) |
| **Core ingredients** | Biotin 5000mcg, Hydrolyzed Marine Collagen 200mg, Keratin 50mg, Zinc 15mg, Vitamin E |

| **Sourcing angle** | White-label from GMP-certified manufacturer (USA/Korea/EU). Custom Nama label in Arabic. |
| **COGS target** | 25–40 SAR/unit at 500+ MOQ |

**Product page hero copy (AR):**
> شعرك ما يحتاج سيروم صيني جديد كل أسبوع. يحتاج تغذية حقيقية من الداخل — بيوتين وكولاجين بحري وكيراتين في أمالج لذيذة تاخذينها كل يوم.

## 4. 180° Positioning & ICP Messaging

### Positioning Statement

> **Nama Beauty is the #1 Saudi brand for inner beauty — because real beauty doesn't come from a tube, it grows from within.**

> **نما بيوتي هي العلامة السعودية الأولى للجمال الداخلي — لأن الجمال الحقيقي ما يجي من أنبوبة، يجي من جواك.**

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Who** | Saudi woman, 22–42, Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam (+ Eastern Province cities) |
| **Income** | Middle to upper-middle (spends 200–500 SAR/month on beauty) |
| **Behavior** | Buys from Instagram/TikTok, checks Nahdi/Noon reviews, influenced by Khaleeji influencers |
| **Pain** | Tried cheap Chinese products, disappointed. Wants something that "feels real" and "looks premium." |
| **Fear** | Wasting money again, being scammed, product not arriving, embarrassing delivery |
| **Desire** | Look good naturally, feel confident without heavy makeup, simple daily ritual |
| **Trigger words (AR)** | عناية، طبيعي، من الداخل، بدون كيماويات، معتمد، ثقة، نما، توهج، راحة، شعر صحي |

### Messaging Framework (Problem → Agitate → Solution → Proof → CTA)

Use this on every product page, collection page, and ad:

```
1. PROBLEM (المشكلة):     Mirror their exact pain in their words
2. AGITATE (التفاقم):     Show what happens if they keep doing nothing
3. SOLUTION (الحل):       Introduce Nama as the inner-beauty approach
4. PROOF (الدليل):        Ingredients, science, reviews, certifications
5. CTA (الخطوة التالية):  اطلبي الآن — الدفع عند الاستلام
```

### Authority & Trust Stack (show on every page)

Layer these in order of visual priority:

1. **"دفع عند الاستلام — بدون مخاطرة"** (COD badge — #1 trust signal in KSA)
2. **"مكوّنات مدروسة علمياً"** with ingredient cards (not vague "natural")
3. **"مصنع في منشأة GMP معتمدة"** (GMP facility badge)
4. **"خالي من: جلوتين، سكر مضاف، مواد حافظة"** (clean label)
5. **Ingredient spotlight sections** — e.g., "ما هو KSM-66؟" with 2-sentence science explanation
6. **Social proof counters** — "أكثر من [X] سيدة سعودية اختارن نما" (update with real numbers post-launch)
7. **Star rating + Arabic reviews** (seed 5–10 real reviews at launch from friends/family beta)
8. **"توصيل سريع داخل السعودية"** with estimated delivery days
9. **FAQ section** addressing COD, ingredients, "هل المنتج حلال؟", "متى أشوف النتائج؟"
10. **Comparison table** — Nama vs. "السيرومات الصينية" vs. "الكريمات المجهولة" (without naming competitors)

## 5. Pricing Architecture

### Standard Pricing (everywhere on site — no discounts)

| Quantity | Price (SAR) | Per Unit | Display |
|----------|-------------|----------|---------|
| 1 product | **199** | 199 | "منتج واحد" |
| 2 products | **279** | 139.5 | "منتجين — وفّري 119 ريال" |
| 3 products | **349** | 116.3 | "٣ منتجات — وفّري 248 ريال" ⭐ BEST VALUE |

**Rules:**
- All cross-sells and upsells in cart drawer, product pages, collection page, and thank you page show **original bundle prices** (199/279/349) — never discounted pre-checkout.
- Bundle selector on product page: customer picks 1, 2, or 3 (if buying from collection, they build their own bundle).
- The "3 for 349" anchor makes 199 feel reasonable and pushes AOV up.

### Post-Checkout Upsell (ONLY discount on entire site)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **When** | After customer enters name + valid KSA phone (starts with 0) and clicks CTA on checkout popup |
| **What** | ONE product at **99 SAR** (limited-time, shown for 10–15 seconds) |
| **Logic** | Smart pick based on cart contents — suggest the product they did NOT buy that is most likely to convert: |
| | • Bought Hair only → offer Rest Gummies (stress causes hair loss) |
| | • Bought Rest only → offer Glow Drink (sleep deprivation dulls skin) |
| | • Bought Glow only → offer Hair Gummies (collagen also helps hair) |
| | • Bought 2 products → offer the missing third |
| | • Bought all 3 → offer another single of their highest-qty item OR skip upsell |
| **Timer** | 15-second countdown with urgency: "عرض خاص — ٩٩ ريال فقط — ينتهي خلال ١٥ ثانية" |
| **If accepted** | Add to order total, show in thank you page summary, send to Google Sheet webhook |
| **If declined / timer expires** | Proceed to thank you page with original order only |
| **CRO copy** | "بما إنك اخترت [product]… جربي [upsell] بسعر خاص ٩٩ ريال — العرض ينتهي الآن" |

### Unit Economics (target)

| Item | SAR |
|------|-----|
| AOV (blended) | ~220 |
| COGS (product + packaging) | ~35 |
| Shipping (local 3PL) | ~18 |
| COD fee | ~12 |
| Ad cost per order (target) | ~40 |
| **Net margin per order** | **~115 SAR (~52%)** |

  ├── Cross-sells (full price): "منتجات أخرى قد تعجبك"
  └── Trust: "لا تدفعين إلا لما يوصلك الطلب"
```

### Checkout Popup CRO Details

- **Order summary visible** — customer sees exactly what they're buying (reduces COD refusal)
- **Only 2 fields** — name + phone. No address yet (confirm city by phone call later to boost completion rate)
- **Phone validation:** Must start with `0`, exactly 10 digits, Saudi mobile prefixes (05x)
- **Example below phone field:** `مثال: 0551234567`
- **COD trust badge** directly above CTA button
- **CTA button text:** "تأكيد الطلب — دفع عند الاستلام" (not just "submit")
- **No email field** (reduces friction; collect later on thank you page optionally)

### Thank You Page CRO Details

- **Confirmation headline:** "تم تأكيد طلبك بنجاح! 🎉"
- **Subheadline:** "راح يتواصل معك فريق نما خلال ساعات لتأكيد التوصيل"
- **Why this helps delivery rate:** Pre-sets expectation that someone WILL call → customer answers phone → confirms → receives
- **Order number** (auto-generated: `NAMA-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`)
- **Full order summary** with prices
- **Cross-sell section:** "منتجات أخرى قد تعجبك" — other products at full 199/279/349 pricing
- **Social proof:** "انضمي لأكثر من [X] سيدة سعودية تثق في نما"
- **Do NOT show** upsell discount on thank you page (that was a one-time checkout offer)

## 10. SFDA Compliance (Critical)

### What You're Selling

All 3 products = **Food Supplements** under SFDA classification (NOT cosmetics, NOT pharmaceuticals).

### Registration Path

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Find GMP manufacturer with existing SFDA-registered similar products | Week 1–2 |
| 2 | White-label with your Nama Arabic label | Week 2–3 |
| 3 | Register via SFDA Electronic Food Registration System (FRCS) | Week 3–8 |
| 4 | Required docs: Free Sale Certificate, GMP cert, lab analysis, Arabic label artwork | — |
| 5 | Use local SFDA agent if needed (~3000–8000 SAR) | — |

### Claims You CAN Use (SFDA-safe)

- "يدعم صحة الشعر" (supports hair health)
- "يساهم في الاسترخاء" (contributes to relaxation)
- "يدعم نضارة البشرة" (supports skin radiance)
- "مكمل غذائي" (food supplement)
- "مصدر للكولاجين / البيوتين / المغنيسيوم" (source of...)

### Claims You CANNOT Use

- ❌ "يعالج الصلع" (treats baldness)
- ❌ "علاج للأرق" (insomnia treatment)
- ❌ "تبييض البشرة" (skin whitening)
- ❌ "ينقص الوزن" (weight loss)
- ❌ "بديل دوائي" (drug alternative)
- ❌ Before/after photos implying medical results

### Shortcut for $500 Budget

Partner with a Saudi supplement brand or importer who already has SFDA registration and offers white-label. You provide the brand (Nama) + label design + sales. They handle compliance. Revenue share or per-unit markup.

## 11. What NOT to Sell (Lessons Learned)

| Category | Why It Fails in KSA COD |
|----------|------------------------|
| Body whitening creams | Low confirmation (shame), low delivery, GHAD paperwork |
| Hair loss serums (minoxidil-type) | Oversold → customers cancel when driver calls |
| Weight loss products | SFDA red flag, ad platform bans, low trust |
| Sexual wellness | Ad bans, delivery embarrassment |
| Generic Chinese gadgets | Race to bottom, 1-star reviews, high RTO |
| Skin serums with drug claims | GHAD cosmetics registration required |
| Anything with "miracle" claims | SFDA rejection + chargebacks |

### The Pattern

> **Oversold Chinese format + embarrassing problem = low confirmation.**
> **Fresh format (gummies, drink, powder) + real problem + premium brand = high confirmation.**
